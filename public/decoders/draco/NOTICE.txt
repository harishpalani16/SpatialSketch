draco_decoder.wasm and draco_wasm_wrapper.js are unmodified glTF decoder
artifacts copied from three 0.186.0, examples/jsm/libs/draco/gltf.
The JavaScript wrapper and WebAssembly binary are served locally; importing
a compressed model does not fetch a decoder from a third-party CDN.
